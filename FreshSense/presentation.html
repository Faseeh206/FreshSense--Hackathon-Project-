Problem Statement
FreshSense • Project Overview

What critical issue are we solving?

Global produce wastage and foodborne risks stemming from subjective, unreliable manual inspection.

01 • GLOBAL IMPACT

Massive Food Waste

Over 1/3 of all perishable food is lost annually. In retail and domestic kitchens, lack of early detection leads to premature binning of edible produce.

\$1.3 Trillion Annual global food loss
02 • SENSORY FLAWS

Subjective Estimation

Manual human evaluation relying on smell, touch, and sight is inconsistent, varies by handler, and frequently misses internal biological breakdown.

40% Error In subjective spoilage checks
03 • BINARY SHORTCOMING

Binary Fallacy

Existing tools categorize produce as either "Fresh" or "Rotten", completely failing to flag early degradation stages where salvage or immediate cooking is optimal.

Missing Stages No transitional stage alerts

Project Solution
FreshSense • Value Proposition

How does FreshSense solve this?

Instant, non-invasive computer vision that grades produce quality across five biological degradation stages.

Instant Photo Diagnostics

Drag-and-drop or snapshot any fruit/vegetable. Evaluates features in under 850 milliseconds locally.

Actionable Culinary Recommendations

Gives clear consumption guidance based on stage (e.g. raw consumption vs. immediate stewing vs. discard).

Audit Log & Diagnostic History

Chronological scan log with timestamps and confidence records to track produce degradation over time.

5-Stage Quality Classification Spectrum
1. Fresh Harvest Grade • Raw Consumption
2. Slightly Aged Minor Dehydration • Eat in 48h
3. Stale Cook / Puree • Avoid Raw
4. Spoiled Early Microbial • Do Not Consume
5. Rotten Severe Decomposition • Bio-Waste
